# Google Veo Cinematographic Prompt Specification
## Project: 10 Evergreen Life Habits (10 Hábitos de Vida Evergreen)

---

## SECTION 1: VISUAL CONTINUITY BIBLE

To maintain absolute visual consistency across all AI-generated video clips in Google Veo, all prompts must strictly adhere to the following baseline visual specifications:

### 1. Key Character (The Presenter)
* **Name / Identity:** Alex, a 32-year-old Latino man with neat short dark brown hair, a well-groomed short beard, warm expressive eyes, and a calm, authoritative demeanour.
* **Wardrobe:** High-quality charcoal grey linen button-down shirt worn open over a plain matte white crew-neck cotton t-shirt. Dark tailored trousers. Minimalist black leather watch on the left wrist. No logos or graphics.

### 2. Primary Environments
* **Presenter Studio (The Evergreen Sanctuary):** A modern minimalist architectural space. Plain warm-toned beige plaster walls, natural light-oak wooden desk, a single potted fiddle-leaf fig plant in a matte ceramic pot. Soft daylight pouring in through a large floor-to-ceiling side window.
* **Modern Home Bedroom:** Minimalist aesthetic, dark oak nightstand, matte white ceramic lamp emitting 2700K warm ambient glow, neutral grey linen bedding.
* **Modern Office Workspace:** Natural light oak wooden desk, clear glass coffee cup, matte black ballpoint pen, A5 beige linen notebook, modern thin bezel laptop with neutral generic interfaces.
* **Urban Outdoor Path:** A serene, tree-lined city walkway with gentle sunlight filtering through green leaves (bokeh effect).

### 3. Key Recurring Props
* **Notebook:** A5-sized beige linen-bound hardcover notebook with blank unlined cream pages.
* **Pen:** Matte black ergonomic metal ballpoint pen.
* **Alarm Clock:** Minimalist matte black digital desk alarm clock with clean white numbers (no brand logos).
* **Mug:** Oatmeal-colored ceramic coffee mug with a matte texture.
* **Smartphone:** Generic bezel-less dark charcoal smartphone with no camera bumps or visible logos.

### 4. Cinematic Color & Aesthetic Style
* **Main Tone:** 8K ultra-photorealistic, shot on Arri Alexa Mini LF, 35mm and 50mm prime lenses, cinematic shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, soft directional lighting.
* **Color Palette:** Warm earthy tones, warm sand beige, rich oak wood, forest green, charcoal grey, and soft daylight highlights. Contrast scenes (stress/distraction) use high-contrast black-and-white or harsh 6000K cold blue light.
